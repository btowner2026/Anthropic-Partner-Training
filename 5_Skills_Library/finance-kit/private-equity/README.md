# 8-Stage Claude Skill Set for Private Equity Deal Teams

30 standalone skills, organised as the eight stages of the private equity deal cycle, from initial screening through exit preparation. Designed for investment professionals who want to use Claude as a rigorous analytical partner across every phase of the deal process.

## Who this is for

PE associates, VPs, and principals who work on deal screening, due diligence, investment committee preparation, and portfolio value creation. Each skill is written in the language of institutional investment: MOIC, IRR, QoE, LBO, DDQ, EBITDA bridge, and IC memo.

## Skills in this pack

Each stage opens with the broad workplan skill for that phase, followed by the specific pieces of work the stage has to produce.

### Stage 1 -- Deal Screening (4 skills)

1. **Deal Screening** -- Rapid proprietary-pipeline triage using a structured scoring rubric, TAM logic, and sponsor-fit assessment to prioritise inbound and proactive deal flow.
2. **Thesis Development** -- Turn a sector interest into a written thesis with a falsifiable core claim, must-be-true conditions, kill criteria agreed in advance, and the entry multiple range the claim supports.
3. **Market Map and Sourcing** -- Map the full target universe of a sub-sector by ownership status and EBITDA size band, scoring thesis fit separately from accessibility, to build a pipeline you chose rather than one you were sent.
4. **Proprietary Angle Assessment** -- Test a claimed proprietary angle against the substitution test, grade it real, partial or none, and price the premium it has to justify against the expected clearing range.

### Stage 2 -- CIM Teardown (3 skills)

5. **CIM Teardown** -- Systematic read-through of a Confidential Information Memorandum, extracting deal thesis, key risks, EBITDA bridge, and management assertions worth stress-testing.
6. **Management Presentation Review** -- Page-by-page diff of the management presentation against the CIM for moved metric definitions, cohorts that stopped being shown, and bridges that do not tie, producing the questions to ask in the session itself.
7. **Information Gap Log** -- Live register of the assumptions the bid depends on but no evidence has yet confirmed, each quantified in EBITDA and turns of entry multiple, with an owner, a need-by date, and the decision it blocks.

### Stage 3 -- Diligence Architecture (4 skills)

8. **Diligence Architecture** -- Build a full due diligence workplan: workstream owners, DDQ templates, key-man risk checklist, and a risk-ranked issues log for the IC memo.
9. **Diligence Workstream Plan** -- Sequence the commercial, financial, operational, legal, tax, and IT workstreams on a critical path against the exclusivity clock, so shape-changing findings arrive before the work they would undo.
10. **Third-Party Advisor Brief** -- Scope an external diligence provider: numbered questions, materiality and de-minimis thresholds, deliverable format, interim read-outs, explicit exclusions, fee phasing, and the reliance position.
11. **Red Flag Register** -- One register in which every finding carries a quantified impact, a disposition of priceable, fixable or walk-away, a named owner, and a status that closes only once the finding has landed in the price, the SPA, or the value creation plan.

### Stage 4 -- Commercial Analysis (4 skills)

12. **Commercial Analysis** -- Market sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM), competitive positioning, customer concentration analysis, and Porter's Five Forces applied to a specific acquisition target.
13. **Customer Cohort Analysis** -- Vintage-level logo, gross, and net revenue retention rebuilt from raw billings, assessed for curve shape and terminal retention, and reconciled to the revenue line in the seller's model.
14. **Pricing Power Assessment** -- Price/volume/mix decomposition of growth, a pocket-price waterfall from list to cash received, contract escalators and caps, and the outcome of the last increase attempted.
15. **Competitive Moat Review** -- Test the business against the named power structures (scale economies, network effects, switching costs, counter-positioning, cornered resource, process power) and state which survive, how long they last relative to the hold, and what terminal margin and exit multiple they support.

### Stage 5 -- Financial Scenario Modelling (4 skills)

16. **Financial Scenario Modelling** -- Build a structured LBO returns analysis with bear/base/bull scenarios, MOIC and IRR sensitivity to entry multiple and leverage, and covenant headroom assessment.
17. **LBO Structuring** -- Sponsor base case with sources and uses that tie, leverage in turns of QoE-adjusted EBITDA, an annual cash sweep, a flat-multiple exit, and returns as both IRR and MOIC with a bridge splitting the equity gain into EBITDA growth, multiple change, and debt paydown.
18. **Debt Package Sizing** -- Rebuild statutory EBITDA addback by addback to the number credit will actually accept, then size tranches, rates, amortisation, and interest and fixed-charge coverage at close and at the trough.
19. **Returns Sensitivity** -- Isolate the two or three variables the return genuinely depends on, present the return surface across them, and state the walk-away price in both entry multiple and enterprise value.

### Stage 6 -- IC Memo Drafting (3 skills)

20. **IC Memo Drafting** -- Investment committee memo structure: deal thesis, business quality summary, diligence findings, risk matrix, returns analysis, and recommendation.
21. **Investment Committee Q&A** -- Question bank ranked by return impact with a written answer to each, the questions that have no good answer labelled as such, a pre-agreed conditions slate, and a speaking plan for who answers what.
22. **Risk and Mitigant Register** -- Every material risk sized in the currency of the deal, matched to one mitigant with a named owner and a date, assessed for what remains after it, and classified as priced, transferred, mitigated, or accepted.

### Stage 7 -- Portfolio Value Creation (4 skills)

23. **Portfolio Value Creation** -- 100-day plan structure, EBITDA bridge decomposition, value creation workstream design, and KPI dashboard for portfolio monitoring.
24. **Hundred-Day Plan** -- A bandwidth budget in executive-days, three to five moves priced against it, a dependency-ordered sequence with the reporting cadence installed in week one, and a published list of what is deliberately not being done.
25. **Operating KPI Cadence** -- The monthly operating review installed as a process: a fixed calendar, eight to twelve precisely defined measures, escalation rules agreed before any variance appears, and an agenda that runs to decisions.
26. **Add-On Acquisition Pipeline** -- Consolidation thesis, target criteria with explicit disqualifiers, the multiple arbitrage arithmetic and the point at which it stops being real, integration capacity stated in deals per year, and a stop rule.

### Stage 8 -- Exit Preparation (4 skills)

27. **Exit Preparation** -- Vendor due diligence readiness, buyer universe mapping, equity story refinement, management presentation structure, and process timeline.
28. **Exit Options Review** -- Trade sale, sponsor-to-sponsor, IPO, continuation vehicle, and continued hold compared on achievable value net to the fund, certainty of completion, time to liquidity, and readiness cost, ending in a primary route, a fallback, and the conditions that switch between them.
29. **Vendor Due Diligence** -- VDD scope by workstream, provider selection and reliance architecture, a working protocol that keeps the sponsor in control of scope without touching conclusions, and the handling of adverse findings, so the pack survives a buyer's confirmatory diligence.
30. **Buyer Narrative and Positioning** -- The three questions the buyer's IC will ask, a de-risking ledger tracking each entry-date risk to its current state, the runway deliberately left for the next owner, and every headline claim graded by proof type.

## How to install

1. Unzip this archive.
2. Add each skill folder to a Claude Project as Project Knowledge (drag the folder into the Knowledge panel in Claude.ai), or copy the SKILL.md files into ~/.claude/skills/ if you are using Claude Code.
3. In Claude, reference the skill by name: "Using the Deal Screening skill, evaluate this inbound opportunity: [paste CIM summary or teaser]."

## Notes

No invented benchmarks, source URLs, or return statistics appear in any of the 30 skills. All frameworks reference named methodologies (MECE, Porter, QoE, LBO structuring conventions) rather than invented proprietary systems.
