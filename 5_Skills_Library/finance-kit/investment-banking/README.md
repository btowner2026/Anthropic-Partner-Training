# 60 Investment Banking Agents for Claude

A pack of 60 ready-to-run Claude agents, one SKILL.md file each, built for investment banking analysts, associates, and deal teams. The agents cover the full arc of a transaction, from the first market read through valuation, execution, financing, negotiation, project management, and post-close monitoring, so a lean team can move faster without dropping institutional rigor. Each agent runs a real, named method (three-statement modeling, DCF with WACC and terminal value, trading comparables, precedent transactions, LBO with IRR and MOIC, accretion and dilution, football-field valuation, fairness opinion ranges, synergy quantification net of cost to achieve, purchase price allocation, earnout ratchets, covenant headroom testing, BATNA and ZOPA negotiation, critical-path timelines, and sensitivity data tables) and returns a structured, client-ready output.

## The 60 agents

### 1. Market and Research Agents
1. **Market Intelligence** (market-intelligence/), a market intelligence brief with catalysts and implications.
2. **Industry Analysis** (industry-analysis/), an industry structure read with attractiveness and risks.
3. **Company Research** (company-research/), a company profile of financials, drivers, and positioning.

### 2. Valuation and Modeling Agents
4. **Financial Modeling** (financial-modeling/), a three-statement model with drivers and checks.
5. **Valuation Analysis** (valuation-analysis/), a football-field valuation range with method drivers.
6. **DCF Modeling** (dcf-modeling/), a DCF with WACC, terminal value, and sensitivities.
7. **Comparable Analysis** (comparable-analysis/), a trading comps set with multiples and a benchmark.
8. **Precedent Transactions** (precedent-transactions/), a precedent deal set with paid multiples and premia.
9. **LBO Modeling** (lbo-modeling/), an LBO model with returns, leverage, and a value bridge.
10. **Fairness Opinion** (fairness-opinion/), a supporting fairness analysis placing the offer against every method range.
11. **Sum-of-the-Parts Valuation** (sum-of-the-parts-valuation/), a segment-by-segment valuation bridged to equity with a holding-company discount.

### 3. M&A Execution Agents
12. **Merger Analysis** (merger-analysis/), an accretion and dilution read with pro-forma effects.
13. **Due Diligence** (due-diligence/), a diligence request list and findings tracker.
14. **CIM / Pitchbook** (cim-pitchbook/), a CIM outline with investment highlights and financials.
15. **Teaser / One Pager** (teaser-one-pager/), a blind teaser with the equity story and no company name.
16. **Buyer Targeting** (buyer-targeting/), a tiered buyer list with fit rationale.
17. **Outreach Email** (outreach-email/), an outreach sequence keyed to buyer tiers.
18. **Synergy Quantification** (synergy-quantification/), a phased synergy build net of cost to achieve, set against the premium paid.
19. **Purchase Price Allocation** (purchase-price-allocation/), an allocation to intangibles and goodwill with the amortization drag on reported earnings.
20. **Earnout Structuring** (earnout-structuring/), an earnout design with its metric, ratchet, cap, and seller governance.
21. **Working Capital Adjustment** (working-capital-adjustment/), a normalised working-capital peg and the completion-accounts mechanism that settles it.
22. **Escrow and Indemnity** (escrow-and-indemnity/), a risk-allocation position with caps, baskets, survival periods, and a sized escrow.
23. **Carve-Out Analysis** (carve-out-analysis/), standalone carve-out EBITDA with separation, transitional service, and stranded costs.

### 4. Deal Management Agents
24. **NDA Tracking** (nda-tracking/), an NDA status log with access and expiry tracking.
25. **Data Room** (data-room/), a data room index and access-tiering plan.
26. **Management Deck** (management-deck/), a management presentation outline and talk track.
27. **Capital Structure** (capital-structure/), a capital structure view with leverage and cost of capital.
28. **Financing Strategy** (financing-strategy/), a financing plan across sources, sizing, and sequence.
29. **Cross-Border Deal Considerations** (cross-border-deal-considerations/), a cross-border read on price currency, withholding, repatriation, and approvals.
30. **Regulatory and Antitrust Review** (regulatory-and-antitrust-review/), a filing map with theories of harm, remedies, and timing to clearance.
31. **Sell-Side Process Letter** (sell-side-process-letter/), a round process letter setting bid form, price basis, and reserved rights.
32. **Deal Pipeline Tracker** (deal-pipeline-tracker/), an origination funnel with stage criteria, conversion, and a weighted forecast.

### 5. Capital Raising Agents
33. **Debt Sourcing** (debt-sourcing/), a lender shortlist with indicative terms.
34. **Equity Sourcing** (equity-sourcing/), an equity investor shortlist with fit rationale.
35. **Term Sheet** (term-sheet/), a term sheet with key terms and their implications.
36. **Syndication** (syndication/), a syndication plan with roles and allocations.
37. **Investor Targeting** (investor-targeting/), a tiered investor target list with angles.
38. **Roadshow** (roadshow/), a roadshow plan with schedule, message, and materials.
39. **IPO Readiness Assessment** (ipo-readiness-assessment/), an IPO readiness read across audit, controls, board, and KPIs, with a gap list and timeline.
40. **Equity Story Development** (equity-story-development/), an equity story with a financial algorithm and the hard objections answered.
41. **Bridge and Acquisition Financing** (bridge-and-acquisition-financing/), a committed financing structure with the bridge, its takeout, and the fee drag.
42. **Rating Agency Presentation** (rating-agency-presentation/), a rating agency case with adjusted metrics, a deleveraging path, and committee Q&A.

### 6. Negotiation and Structuring Agents
43. **Q&A Preparation** (qa-preparation/), a Q&A prep pack with hard questions and answers.
44. **Objection Handling** (objection-handling/), an objection playbook with responses and proof.
45. **Offer Evaluation** (offer-evaluation/), a bid comparison scoring price, certainty, and terms.
46. **Negotiation Support** (negotiation-support/), a negotiation plan with levers, BATNA, and ZOPA.
47. **Deal Structuring** (deal-structuring/), a deal structure across consideration, earnouts, and protections.
48. **Management Incentive Plan** (management-incentive-plan/), a post-close equity pool with vesting, ratchet, and leaver terms.

### 7. Project Management Agents
49. **Workstream Coordinator** (workstream-coordinator/), a workstream map with owners and dependencies.
50. **Timeline & Milestones** (timeline-milestones/), a deal timeline with milestones and the critical path.
51. **Task Management** (task-management/), a prioritized task tracker with owners and due dates.
52. **Quality Control** (quality-control/), a QC checklist and review log for deliverables.

### 8. Governance and Monitoring Agents
53. **Compliance** (compliance/), a compliance checklist covering information walls and conflicts.
54. **Risk Assessment** (risk-assessment/), a deal risk register with likelihood, impact, and mitigations.
55. **Scenario Analysis** (scenario-analysis/), a set of scenarios with value and decision implications.
56. **Sensitivity Analysis** (sensitivity-analysis/), sensitivity ranges across the key value drivers.
57. **Reporting** (reporting/), a deal status report with progress, risks, and next steps.
58. **Post-Deal Monitoring** (post-deal-monitoring/), a post-close tracker of synergies, integration, and value.
59. **Knowledge Base** (knowledge-base/), a structured knowledge capture of precedents and lessons.
60. **Covenant Package Analysis** (covenant-package-analysis/), rebuilt covenant EBITDA with basket capacity and headroom under a downside case.

## Install

Unzip, then add the folders to a Claude Project as Project Knowledge, or drop them into `~/.claude/skills/`. Name the agent in your prompt to run it.
